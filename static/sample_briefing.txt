ANTHROPIC: PRE-CALL BRIEFING

OVERVIEW
Anthropic is an AI safety and research company headquartered in San Francisco. It was founded in 2021 by former OpenAI researchers.

LEADERSHIP
Dario Amodei is the CEO and co-founder of Anthropic. Daniela Amodei is the President and co-founder. Mike Krieger joined Anthropic as Chief Product Officer in 2024.

FUNDING AND FINANCIALS
Amazon has invested $8 billion in Anthropic in total. Anthropic's most recent funding round was a Series E in March 2025, which raised $3.5 billion at a $61.5 billion valuation. Anthropic's annualized revenue run rate is about $1 billion.
